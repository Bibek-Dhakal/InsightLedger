# Recommendation Memo: 1-Click Checkout Experiment

**Action:** Roll out the 1-click checkout feature to 100% of users.

**Expected Impact:**
Based on our experiment, the treatment group showed an absolute conversion lift. Assuming current traffic volumes hold,
we anticipate an approximate relative increase of ~25% in overall successful checkout conversions.

**Uncertainty & Confidence Intervals:**
The 95% Confidence Interval for the absolute difference confirms the statistical significance of the lift. There is no
evidence of Sample Ratio Mismatch (p > 0.05).

**Assumptions & Caveats:**

- The sample collected over the test duration is representative of seasonal traffic.
- Future performance may fluctuate slightly depending on variations in user demographics over different months.
